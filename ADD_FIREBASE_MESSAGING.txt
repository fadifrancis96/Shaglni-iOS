🔥 QUICK SETUP: Add FirebaseMessaging to Xcode

1. Open Xcode project
2. Click on project name "Shaglni" in navigator
3. Select target "Shaglni"
4. Go to tab "General"
5. Scroll to "Frameworks, Libraries, and Embedded Content"
6. Click the "+" button at the bottom
7. In the search box, type: FirebaseMessaging
8. Select "FirebaseMessaging" from the list
9. Click "Add"

Then click Build (Cmd+B) to verify it compiles!

Note: The package dependency is already resolved, you just need to link it to your target.
